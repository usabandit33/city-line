Questions people ask when the permit line looks high

Can a Phoenix contractor charge more than the city permit fee?

The city table is one number. Filing, corrections, and inspection time are other work. Ask the quote to show the city fee and the admin time as separate lines.

Where is the City of Phoenix fee published?

Planning and Development fees sit in the Phoenix city code fee schedule. They move with project valuation and work type. Confirm the current table for your job. This site does not invent a dollar figure.

Is a markup on a permit legal in Arizona?

Arizona does not publish a statewide cap on contractor admin for pulling a permit. The written contract is what you agreed to. Ask for the city receipt. This page is not legal advice.

Who should pull the permit?

Many jobs need the licensed contractor of record. Some owner-builder paths exist. Ask which path applies before demolition or rough-in.

Do Phoenix suburbs use the same fee table?

No. Phoenix, Mesa, Scottsdale, and unincorporated Maricopa County use different schedules. Write the city or county on the quote.

What should be in writing?

City fee, plan-review add-on if any, who files, and any separate admin charge — before you sign. Phoenix phone numbers are listed so you can ask the shop the same questions.
