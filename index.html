The city fee is public. Ask whether the permit line on your quote is that fee or something else.

Phoenix homeowners often see one “permit” number that is several times the Planning and Development table. Use this list before you sign.

  • Look up the City of Phoenix (or your suburb) published fee for that class of work.
  • Ask the contractor to split the city fee from admin time (file, correct plans, meet the inspector).
  • Ask who pulls the permit — the licensed contractor, a runner, or you.
  • Name the jurisdiction. Phoenix, Mesa, Scottsdale, and Maricopa County are not the same table.
  • Ask for the split in writing before you sign, including any plan-review add-on.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website and run through this list.
